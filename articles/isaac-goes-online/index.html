Isaac Goes Online on Consoles

← all articles

Tap any word to see the translation.

The Binding of Isaac is coming to consoles in a brand-new way. On November 19, 2026, a version called Repentance+ Online will launch on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S and Nintendo Switch 2. The date is a big deal, because it is also the release day of GTA VI, so this small roguelike will compete with one of the biggest games ever made.

The main new feature is online co-op. Up to four players can team up and fight their way through the basement together. The package also includes the whole game with all the expansions that have appeared over the years, so newcomers get everything in one place.

PC players already know much of this. Online multiplayer reached PC in December 2024 as a beta, and the reception was mixed: some players complained about bugs and poor performance. Early reviews of the Switch 2 version suggest that many of these problems have been fixed.

Fans who like boxes on a shelf have just one option. A physical copy for Switch 2 is listed for $69.99 at one retailer, while every console will also get a digital version. The game was created by Edmund McMillen, and it has stayed popular for more than a decade, mostly because every single run is different.

Whether you play alone or with friends, one thing is certain: the basement is about to get a lot more crowded.
